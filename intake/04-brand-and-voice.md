## Writing habits to keep or drop
- **Lead with a lesson that cost you something** rather than a skill list. Which lessons are yours? [...]
- **Numbers:** use real, rough figures ("a table of over two million rows") over round marketing ones. Which numbers can you stand behind? [...]
- **Perfect parallel lists** ("fast, reliable, scalable") read as generated. Tell us if you want them avoided: [yes / no]
- **Own your tools:** if you use AI or code generation in your work, say how. Your statement: [...]
- **Experience framing:** how do you describe your seniority honestly? [e.g. "primary engineer on a small team"]
